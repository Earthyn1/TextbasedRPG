# MUDOSRS — Story & World Notes

## Tone & Vision
- **Not a happy anime adventure.** Realistic, dark, grounded.
- Adventuring is genuinely dangerous — death is common and present.
- The city is built on top of something ancient and threatening.
- Institutional corruption is everywhere, dressed up as order and faith.

---

## The World

### The City
- A **tourist destination** built around the dungeon beneath it.
- Rich visitors come to **bet on adventurers' lives** — gladiatorial but with a veneer of respectability. Spectators never have to look the adventurers in the eye.
- The whole economy and culture exists because of the dungeon. The city *profits* from it.
- **Dungeon magic is seeping upward** through the foundations. The city's establishment is suppressing this news because it would hurt tourism and betting revenue.

### The Church
- Corrupt and powerful. **Charges high prices for healing** — the people meant to be charitable are squeezing the desperate for coin.
- Aligned with the wealthy class, taking a cut of the adventuring economy while staying clean and safe above ground.
- Controls a fundamental law of the world: **magic and physical weapons do not mix** — *unless* you are a sanctioned Holy Paladin.
  - Paladins require church membership, training fees, tithes. It's a monopoly on power dressed as doctrine.
- If they discover an unsanctioned person combining aether and weapons, they would **stop at nothing to kill them**.

### Class Divide
- Rich tourists and gamblers at the top.
- Adventurers, stable hands, and support workers at the bottom.
- The player starts firmly at the bottom.

## The Player Character

### Origin
- Arrives in the city to **become an adventurer out of necessity** — sick parents, no income, no other options.
- Not here for glory. Here because they're desperate.
- Starts working at the **Adventurer's Stable** to earn coin while preparing to attempt the dungeon.

### The Aether Awakening (Two-Stage)

**Stage 1 — The Rat Cellar**
- Early quest: clear rats from a blacksmith's cellar.
- Player spots a **hole in the wall with purple mist** seeping through.
- Stupidly sticks their head in to peek — inhales the mist.
- *Nothing changes. Nothing dramatic happens.* Just noted quietly.
- Player is instructed to report this to the Adventurers Guild — planting seeds of who is tracking the seepage and why.

**Stage 2 — The Flooded Sewer**
- Later mission. Player is accompanied by a companion they met earlier.
- They encounter a **much stronger monster** in a flooded sewer.
- Player loses the fight. About to die.
- In desperation and hatred, something **tears out of them** — raw aether energy — and kills the monster.
- Not triumphant. Terrifying. *What was that.*
- The companion witnesses everything. They're alive because of it.

---

## The Companion

- Met organically before the sewer mission, helps the player reach the entrance.
- Witnesses the aether event. Her reaction is **fear, not awe**.
- Immediately understands the stakes — tells the player: if the church finds out, they will kill you.
- Her knowing this so readily hints at a personal history — a family member silenced, someone she knew who had an unsanctioned ability?
- Becomes the player's **first real confidant** — the only person who knows their secret.
- Creates a parallel story structure: the official adventuring life, and the hidden one running underneath it.
- Background TBD — clearly not naive about how the city actually works.

## Key Themes
- Desperation as motivation (not destiny)
- Power that comes from trauma, not training
- Institutional rot beneath a respectable surface
- The city that profits from danger, now threatened by it
- A secret that makes you a target of the most powerful organisation in the world
